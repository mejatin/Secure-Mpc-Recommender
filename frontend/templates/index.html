Privacy

A single party's share is uniformly random over the field — it reveals nothing about the real value on its own.

⚡

Distributed

Each party runs its own share of the computation locally, coordinating only through DPF-masked exchanges.

🛡️

Threat model

Compromising one party does not reveal the underlying profile — security holds as long as the two parties don't collude.

Security model: two-party additive secret sharing with a semi-honest, non-colluding assumption between the two parties.
